Welcome to Your Groovebox

The MicroCycles Groovebox is a compact beat-making instrument designed for live performance and creative experimentation. Think of it as a combination drum machine and sequencer—six tracks, 16 steps, and powerful real-time control.

What you can do with it:

  • Create drum patterns with up to 16 steps per track
  • Control six different drum and percussion samples
  • Adjust how hard each drum hit sounds and when it happens
  • Chain patterns together to build complete songs
  • Save your work and perform live
  • Swap sample sets to change your drum kit instantly

This manual will guide you through every feature—from switching it on for the first time to building complex arrangements for live performance.
