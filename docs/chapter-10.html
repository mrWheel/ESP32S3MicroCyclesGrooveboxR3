Mute

What is Mute?

Mute allows you to silence a specific step without deleting it or losing its settings. When a step is muted, it will not play, but all its parameters (Velocity, Pitch, Decay, Probability) are preserved.

This is useful for creating variations on patterns, turning sounds on and off during performance, or temporarily disabling a step while keeping its configuration.

Mute Values

Value What It Does When to Use
OFF Step plays normally All steps by default; step is active
ON Step is silenced (won't play) When you want to disable a step temporarily or create variations

How to Adjust Mute

  1. Select the track you want to edit on the main screen
  2. Short Press the encoder to enter track-edit mode
  3. Rotate the encoder until you are on the step you want to edit
  4. Medium Press the encoder to enter Step Edit mode
  5. Rotate the encoder to cycle through the popup items until you see "MUTE"
  6. Short Press encoder (cursor moves to the value field)
  7. Rotate the encoder to toggle between OFF and ON
  8. OFF = step plays, ON = step is silenced
  9. Short press encoder to accept selected value
  10. Short press KEY0 to exit step edit mode and return to track-edit mode

Using the Edit Popup

When you press medium press, a popup appears showing all editable parameters for the selected step:

Edit Step 5
    VELOCITY     100
    PITCH        +00
    DECAY        50%
    PROBABILITY  100%
   >MUTE<        OFF
    CHAIN   NO
  1. Navigate between parameters: Rotate the encoder to scroll through Velocity, Pitch, Decay, Probability, Mute, and Chain. The highlighted parameter is between `>..<`.
  2. Edit a parameter: When a parameter is highlighted, press the encoder (short press) to enter edit mode
  3. Change the value: Rotate the encoder to toggle OFF/ON: 
    Edit Step 5
    VELOCITY     100
    PITCH        +00
    DECAY        50%
    PROBABILITY  100%
    MUTE         >ON<
    CHAIN   NO
  4. Confirm the value: Press the encoder (short press) to lock in the new value and return to parameter selection
  5. Continue editing: Repeat steps 1-4 to adjust other parameters
  6. Close the popup: Press KEY0 (short press) to close the popup. Your changes are saved.

Practical Uses

Create Pattern Variations: Keep step settings but mute them to create a simpler version of a pattern. Unmute them to bring back complexity.

Live Performance: During a performance, mute certain steps to create fill effects or build/drop moments without stopping the pattern.

Troubleshooting Mix Clarity: If multiple sounds are playing and the mix sounds muddy, try muting some background sounds to see if that clears things up.

Quick Off/On: Faster than deleting and re-creating a step—just toggle mute OFF or ON.
